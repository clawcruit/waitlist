1What is Clawcruit?

Clawcruit is a multi-tenant platform for hiring, onboarding, and managing autonomous artificial intelligent employees. Each hire runs in its own isolated workspace with persistent "sovereign brain" made of editable Markdown files. The product is developed by ParagonAI Labs and is powered by the OpenClaw autonomous-agent framework for channel synchronization, tool use, and core runtime behavior.

Instead of subscribing to another piece of software, you pick or describe a role and Clawcruit builds and onboards an autonomous employee for that role in about 60 seconds. That employee, a "Clawcruit," works from your dashboard and from the chat apps your team already uses, guided by a knowledge base that you write, edit, and fully control.

2What does Clawcruit do?

Clawcruit lets you create an AI hire, give it a professional role, supply business knowledge, connect it to the chat tools your team already uses, and start receiving work from it. The hire works inside your business, follows internal workflows, escalates when needed, and keeps learning from the knowledge you provide. Most of the real-time chat, channel routing, and tool-calling capability is delivered by OpenClaw under the hood.

3Who can use Clawcruit?

Clawcruit can be used by owners, founders, and employers of any team size. A solo founder hiring their first employee, and an established business filling a role that's expensive or hard to staff, are both squarely who Clawcruit is built for.

Because a role is something you describe rather than something you pick off a fixed menu, Clawcruit isn't limited to one industry or company size. If you have the job description, Clawcruit can build the employee.

4What gap is Clawcruit filling?

Hiring physical humans for many operational roles is slow, expensive, and administratively heavy. Onboarding can take weeks while salary already runs. Knowledge leaves when people leave. Performance varies. Management overhead consumes strategic time. Capacity is hard to scale up or down quickly. These are problems employers deal with quietly, long before anyone calls them a "workforce crisis."

Clawcruit addresses that exact set of problems: it offers capacity that is fast to start, usage-based in cost, consistent in output, retains knowledge inside the workspace, and requires far less day-to-day people management while maintaning output.

5Clawcruit use cases

The full shape of the product, in eleven parts.

a. Setup

You create an account, name the hire, describe your business, choose a role (or define a custom one), and connect one or more channels. The workspace and brain files are provisioned automatically. The whole flow is designed to take roughly 60 seconds for a first working agent. A short orientation conversation afterward fills in the rest of what it needs to know.

b. Built-in expertise (Professional roles)

Clawcruit ships with 50 ready-made professional role templates across 32 departments — Sales, Customer Support, Finance, Human Resources, Legal, Marketing, IT Support, Technical Support, Quality Assurance, Risk Management, and more. A sample:

  • Sales Development Representative
  • Customer Support Specialist
  • Accounts Payable Specialist
  • Executive Assistant
  • DevOps Monitor
  • HR Operations Specialist
  • Content Marketing Manager

Each preloaded with role-specific workflows as a starting point, refined by your own business information. You can also define completely custom roles. See the full role directory for every department and role available today.

c. Chat channels

Hires can operate inside the tools your team already uses. Supported channels (powered by the OpenClaw Gateway) include WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, iMessage and others. An "allow list" helps select who instructs your Clawcruit among the team inside the chat channel. The same hire can also be reached through the default Clawcruit web dashboard chatbox. You do not have to force a new app on your team.

d. Clawcruit onboarding process

The guided flow covers identity, company context, role selection, channel connection, and final review. After launch, an orientation conversation helps the hire receive initial documents, URLs, or first tasks so it starts with real business context rather than a blank slate.

e. Web dashboard & security

The dashboard is the control centre for your Clawcruit hires, knowledge base editing, channel settings, and account security. Authentication uses short-lived JWTs with rotating HttpOnly refresh tokens. File access is restricted by an explicit allowlist. Domain validation can limit where API keys may be used. Rate limiting and HTTPS are enforced in production. Every workspace's data is isolated from every other customer's.

f. MCP and API keys (Connecting to your existing tools)

Clawcruit can operate the software you already run (CRMs, ticketing systems, internal tools, etc.) via MCP-style tool connection or a simple API key, so it can act inside your tools rather than just describe what it would do.

g. Sovereign brain files

Every Clawcruit hire is defined by three persistent, human-readable Markdown files that live inside its isolated workspace:

  • SOUL.md — identity, tone, core directives, and escalation rules
  • BOOTSTRAP.md — business knowledge
  • SKILLS.md — role-specific workflows and protocols

You can edit these files in real time from the Knowledge Base tab on your Dashboard screen. Changes take effect on the next response. The knowledge stays with the workspace even if people leave the company.

h. Languages

Clawcruit understands major global and regional languages out of the box, and can be taught additional ones as your business needs them.

i. Custom roles

If none of the built-in roles fit, describe the role and its responsibilities — Clawcruit builds a tailored SKILLS.md around exactly what you need.

j. Model switching

The platform routes simple messages (greetings, short confirmations, status checks) to faster, lower-cost models and reserves higher-capability models for complex reasoning, analysis, or negotiation. When remaining token quota is low, the system can force the cheaper tier automatically to protect cost.

k. Human hand-off & escalation

Built-in triggers (a refund request, a legal mention, a customer asking for a manager, or genuine uncertainty) automatically flag a conversation for a human teammate instead of letting Clawcruit guess.

8FAQs

What is Clawcruit in simple terms?

Clawcruit is a platform that lets you hire an autonomous AI employee in about a minute. You give it a role and your business knowledge; it then works inside the chat apps your team already uses and can be managed from a web dashboard. It is built on the OpenClaw agent framework and packaged so non-technical teams can use it without managing servers or writing code.

How accurate is Clawcruit?

Clawcruit answers from the business knowledge you give it in BOOTSTRAP.md. When it doesn't have enough information to answer confidently, it's built to say so and escalate to a human manager rather than guess. Accuracy scales directly with how complete your knowledge base is.

Does Clawcruit and its partners train AI models on my data?

No. ParagonAI Labs does not use your workspace data, conversation history, or uploaded documents to train AI models. Messages are sent to the model provider (currently the Google Gemini family or successors) for inference only. We configure the integration so that, by default, provider training on your content is disabled. Enterprise customers can request zero-data-retention options where the provider offers them.

How long does it take to onboard a Clawcruit?

The core onboarding flow is designed to take approximately 60 seconds: name the hire, describe the business, pick a role, connect channels, and launch. You can then continue feeding it documents, URLs, and instructions so its knowledge deepens over the following hours and days. Clawcruit actively learns from daily tasks and instructions as your business evolves.

How do I talk to, instruct, or interact with my Clawcruit?

You interact with it the same way you talk to a colleague: in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, any other connected channel, or the default web dashboard chatbox. No special commands — plain language works.

Can Clawcruit read documents and images?

Yes to documents today — upload a PDF, DOCX, XLSX, or TXT file and Clawcruit folds it directly into its knowledge base. Same is to most image formats including PNG, JPG, and JPEG. Clawcruit can also access webpages to find and read information.

Can Clawcruit prepare documents and visuals?

Yes, through tool-driven provider backends. Clawcruit can generate images, short videos, and audio/music from text prompts or reference inputs. It can process raw data sets and output formatted Markdown reports with tables or write Python scripts (using libraries like Matplotlib or Plotly) to compile visual charts.

Can Clawcruit handle or use my existing software and tools?

Yes — this is a core direction of the product. Through API keys and Model Context Protocol (MCP)–style integrations, Clawcruit hires can be given controlled access to the software your business already runs (CRMs, internal APIs, spreadsheets, ticketing systems, etc.). The hire is then no longer limited to chat; it can take actions inside the tools you authorise.

How do I connect my Clawcruit to my software or tools?

From your dashboard, add the tool as an MCP server connection, or supply an API endpoint and key. Once connected, Clawcruit can call on it automatically whenever you instruct it.

How do I link my Clawcruit to my team?

Add it to your existing WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, or Discord groups the same way you'd add a new teammate. Via "allow_list", you can restrict it to only act on instructions from the team leads you designate.

How does Clawcruit benefit its users?

Clawcruit has a long array of benefits to its users ranging from refined employee onboarding, easy and active training, knowledge retention, workforce management, and administratively affordable remuneration. Users gain capacity that starts in minutes instead of weeks, costs that scale with actual usage instead of fixed headcount, consistent availability, knowledge that stays inside the company, and significantly lower day-to-day management overhead for the roles the agents cover.

What type of business can use Clawcruit?

Any business with a role it needs filled — from a solo founder hiring their first employee to an established team filling a role that's expensive or hard to staff.

Is Clawcruit actually more affordable or efficient than hiring a human?

For many operational roles, yes. A physical hire carries fixed monthly cost (salary, benefits, taxes, tools), weeks of ramp-up, management overhead, and the risk that knowledge leaves when the person leaves. Clawcruit capacity is usage-based, starts in minutes, retains knowledge in the workspace, and does not require the same people-management load. The exact saving depends on the role, volume of work, and how well the agent is configured, but the structural cost and speed advantages are the core reason the product exists.
