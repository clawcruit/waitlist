Clawcruit is an autonomous AI workforce platform developed and operated by ParagonAI Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”), a company to be registered under the laws of Kenya with headquarters in Mombasa, Kenya.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data when you use the Clawcruit platform, including the web dashboard, API, web widget, waitlist, and any connected messaging channels (collectively, the “Service”).

By using Clawcruit, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account data

When you create a Clawcruit account or join the waitlist, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (if provided)
  • bcrypt-hashed password (we never store passwords in plain text)
  • Subscription plan and billing status (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise)
  • Account creation and last-login timestamps

2.2 Workspace and agent data

Each Clawcruit account is associated with a sovereign workspace — an isolated directory containing:

  • SOUL.md — agent identity and tone configuration
  • BOOTSTRAP.md — business knowledge base
  • SKILLS.md — role and capability definitions
  • config.json — security settings (authorised domains, notification preferences)
  • HEARTBEAT.log — agent activity logs (status, last action, token usage)
  • user.json — account metadata

You own this data. It is never shared with other users or used to train AI models.

2.3 Conversation and message data

Messages processed by your Clawcruit agent (including customer messages received via WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, iMessage, or the web widget) are:

  • Passed to the Google Gemini API (or successor model providers) for processing
  • Logged in your workspace’s HEARTBEAT.log for activity tracking
  • Not stored by us in a centralised conversation database outside your workspace

2.4 Uploaded documents

Documents you upload (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, etc.) are stored only in your sovereign workspace, parsed into your knowledge base, and never shared outside that workspace.

2.5 Technical and usage data

We automatically collect:

  • Server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp, HTTP status)
  • Error logs for debugging
  • Token usage estimates for the fuel-gauge feature
  • Basic device/browser information necessary for security and session management

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or data brokers. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

2.6 Categories of personal information (California – CCPA/CPRA)

In the preceding 12 months we may have collected the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers (email, account ID, IP address)
  • Commercial information (subscription plan, usage metrics)
  • Internet or electronic network activity (access logs, token usage)
  • Professional or employment-related information (if you supply company/role details)
  • Inferences drawn from the above solely to operate the Service

We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined under the CPRA for the purpose of inferring characteristics, nor do we sell or share personal information.

4. How we use your data

We use your data solely to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session (JWT-based: 15-minute access tokens with 7-day rotating HttpOnly refresh tokens)
  • Operate, improve, and secure your Clawcruit agent and workspace
  • Process messages sent to your agent through connected channels
  • Send escalation alerts to your registered contact number when human intervention is required
  • Calculate token quota usage and enforce plan limits
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not use your workspace data, conversation history, or uploaded documents to train AI models.

5. Data sovereignty, storage and international transfers

Your workspace data is stored on infrastructure we or you provision (VPS or managed cloud).

Because we use the Google Gemini API (and may use other model providers), some message content is processed on servers located in the United States or other countries outside Kenya and the EEA.

When personal data is transferred outside Kenya or the EEA/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent transfer mechanisms adopted by the European Commission / UK authorities where required
  • Provider contractual commitments regarding data protection and zero-data-retention options where available for enterprise customers

You can export or permanently delete your entire workspace at any time from Settings → Danger Zone. Deletion removes all files under your workspace directory and removes your email from the lookup index. This action is irreversible.

6. Third-party services

6.1 Google Gemini API (or successor models)

Messages are sent to Google’s Generative AI services for inference. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to that processing. We configure the integration so that, by default, Google does not use your content to train its models. Enterprise customers may request a key under Google’s Zero Data Retention terms where available.

6.2 OpenClaw Gateway and messaging platforms

Channel synchronisation is powered by the OpenClaw Gateway and the respective platform APIs (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, etc.). Their privacy policies and developer terms also apply to data in transit.

6.3 No advertising or analytics networks

Clawcruit does not integrate advertising networks, third-party behavioural analytics trackers, or data brokers.

7. Security

We implement the following measures:

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12)
  • Short-lived JWTs (15 min) with rotating HttpOnly refresh tokens (7 days)
  • Explicit file-access allowlist (only designated brain files are readable/writable via the API)
  • Domain validation for API keys
  • Rate limiting on authentication and other endpoints
  • HTTPS/TLS required in production
  • Server access and error logging with limited retention

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@paragonailabs.com.
